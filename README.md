## 🎨 Resource Packs

**See a pack before you use it.** Pick a pack and walk around it in 3D — blocks, armour, swords, items and the HUD, exactly as they look in game.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://breached.gg/showcase/pack-showcase-1920.webp" alt="Breached resource pack 3D preview" width="900">
</div>

- 🧪 **The Mixer** — pick textures one by one from every pack you own. Sword from one, grass from another, armour and sky from others. Breached saves the result as a new pack, ready to use.
- 🕹️ **Live 3D preview** — WASD to walk, drag to look, scroll to zoom. Swap your held item and armour to check every texture.
- 🗂️ **Built for big collections** — folders, search, and a list of what's in use. The top pack wins when two change the same thing.
